# GigPro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

GigPro is an offline-first job management app for sole traders and small service businesses.

## Data Collection
GigPro does NOT collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

All data you enter (jobs, customers, invoices, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

## Internet Usage
GigPro may access the internet only for:
- Weather lookups (based on device location, if permitted)
- Opening external links (e.g. Google Maps)

No data is sent to any backend servers owned by the developer.

## Permissions
GigPro may request:
- Notifications (for job reminders)
- Location (optional, for weather only)
- Storage (to save invoices and backups locally)

All permissions are optional and controlled by the user.

## Third Parties
GigPro does not share data with third parties.
No analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs are used.
