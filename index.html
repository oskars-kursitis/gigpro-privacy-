Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 July 2026  ·  app.gigpro.manager

GigPro is a local-first app. Your jobs, invoices, customers, and financial data are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive, store, or process your personal business data.

What data we collect

GigPro does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data you enter — jobs, invoices, customers, expenses, and overheads — is saved locally on your device using standard Android storage.

The only external services the app may contact are:

  • Open-Meteo — an open-source weather API used to show forecast temperatures on the job calendar. Your approximate location is sent to this API to fetch local weather. Open-Meteo does not store or sell location data. No account is required.
  • Xero (optional) — if you choose to connect a Xero account, an OAuth token is stored securely on your device. Invoice data is sent to Xero only when you explicitly push an invoice.
  • RevenueCat (optional) — if you subscribe to GigPro Pro, purchase transactions are processed via RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy governs that data.

Location

GigPro requests your device location solely to fetch weather for the job calendar. Location is not stored, logged, or shared with any third party beyond the Open-Meteo weather request. You may deny location permission; the weather feature will simply not appear.

Backups and exports

GigPro can create backup files and CSV exports. These files are saved to your device and shared only when you explicitly use the share function. We have no access to these files. Where you send them is governed by those services' own policies.

Notifications

GigPro may send local notifications (for example, job reminders). These are generated on-device and are not routed through any external service.

Children

GigPro is intended for use by adults operating as self-employed tradespeople or gig workers. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete all app data by uninstalling GigPro or by clearing the app's storage in your device settings. No request to us is needed.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of GigPro after changes constitutes acceptance.
